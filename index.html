THE IDEA

A clearer conversation can start before the consultation.

People may know what they are feeling but struggle to organise the details or find the words they want to use.

IsoHealthGuide SA is designed to help people prepare in their own words, so they can take a clearer starting point into a conversation with a healthcare professional.

◌

01 / COMMUNICATION

Finding the words

It can be difficult to explain a concern clearly, especially when someone is unsure where to begin.

⌁

02 / PREPARATION

Organising details

People may forget important details or questions they wanted to raise during an appointment.

文

03 / LANGUAGE

Language and comfort

People may feel more comfortable describing their concerns in a familiar language.

These are product-design hypotheses to validate with patients and healthcare professionals.

HOW IT WORKS

Five steps towards a clearer conversation.

A guided preparation journey designed around a familiar mobile channel.

01

Open

Start from a clinic QR code or the service's WhatsApp entry point.

02

Choose

Select an available language and review privacy information and consent.

03

Prepare

Respond to guided prompts in your own words.

04

Review

Check the summary, make corrections and decide whether to use it.

05

Connect

Take your approved summary into your consultation.

Urgent concerns must not wait for an ordinary chatbot questionnaire. Clinical safety and escalation pathways must be reviewed before live use.

FOR HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS

A clearer starting point for the conversation.

IsoHealthGuide SA is being developed to support patient preparation and communication—not to replace clinical judgement or the consultation itself.

  • Structured information based on patient responses
  • Patient review and correction before sharing
  • A design that recognises language needs
  • Clinic workflows shaped with healthcare partners

OUR BOUNDARIES

Support the conversation. Never replace care.

Trust must be built into the product—not added as an afterthought.

+ We help organise People can prepare what they want to discuss.
+ People stay in control Review, correct and choose whether to share.
− We do not diagnose Clinical decisions belong to qualified professionals.
− We do not replace emergency care The service must never delay urgent help.
