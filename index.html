THE IDEA

A more confident conversation can start before the consultation.

People may know what they are feeling but struggle to organise the details or find the words they want to use. Language, unfamiliar terms and the pressure of an appointment can make that harder.

IsoHealthGuide SA is designed to help people prepare in their own words—so they can take a clearer starting point into a conversation with a healthcare professional.

01
◌

Finding the words

It can be difficult to explain a concern clearly, especially when someone is unsure where to begin.

02
⌁

Organising details

People may forget important details or questions they wanted to raise during an appointment.

03
文

Language and comfort

People may feel more comfortable describing their concerns in a familiar language.

These are product-design hypotheses to validate with patients, healthcare professionals and clinic partners.

HOW IT IS DESIGNED TO WORK

Five steps towards a clearer conversation.

A guided preparation journey, designed for a familiar mobile channel.

01▦

Open

Start from a clinic QR code or the service's WhatsApp entry point.

02文

Choose

Select an available language and review the privacy information and consent request.

03☷

Prepare

Respond to guided prompts in your own words. The assistant helps organise, not diagnose.

04✓

Review

Check the summary, make corrections and decide whether you want to use it.

05↗

Connect

Take your approved summary into your consultation or share it through an approved process.

!

Urgent concerns must not wait for an ordinary chatbot questionnaire. The final product will require clinically reviewed safety and escalation pathways before real-world use.

FOR HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS

A clearer starting point for the conversation.

IsoHealthGuide SA is being developed to support patient preparation and communication—not to replace clinical judgement or the consultation itself.

  • Structured information based on patient responses
  • Patient review and correction before sharing
  • A design that recognises language needs

OUR BOUNDARIES

Support the conversation.
Never replace care.

Trust must be built into the product—not added as an afterthought.

+

We aim to help organise. People can prepare what they want to discuss.

+

People stay in control. Review, correct and choose whether to share.

−

We do not diagnose or prescribe. Clinical decisions belong to qualified healthcare professionals.

−

We do not replace emergency care. The service must not delay urgent help.
