The idea behind IsoHealthGuide

Healthcare conversations should begin with understanding.

Language barriers and uncertainty can make it difficult to explain what is happening. We are developing a simple digital experience to help people prepare for that conversation.

◎

Speak comfortably

Patients should be able to start organising their concerns in a language they are comfortable using.

✳

Make sense of concerns

A guided conversation can help organise a patient's own descriptions into a clearer structure.

↗

Prepare for the consultation

A patient-reviewed summary can help support communication with a healthcare professional.

The patient journey

From uncertainty to a clearer conversation.

The intended experience is simple, guided and focused on helping patients prepare for a healthcare consultation.

01 / START

Open the conversation

The patient accesses the service through a participating healthcare provider's WhatsApp entry point.

02 / CHOOSE

Select a language

The patient chooses an available language for the guided experience.

03 / EXPLAIN

Describe concerns

Guided prompts help the patient organise the information they want to communicate.

04 / PREPARE

Review a summary

The patient reviews the prepared summary before choosing whether to share it with a provider.

For healthcare providers

Support the conversation. Keep care human.

IsoHealthGuide SA is being developed as a communication-support tool that could help patients arrive better prepared to explain their concerns.

  • ✓Structured patient-reported information.
  • ✓A patient review step before information is shared.
  • ✓A tool designed to complement, not replace, professional judgement.
Our responsibility

Technology should know its limits.

IsoHealthGuide SA is intended to support communication and preparation — not to make medical decisions. Clinical care remains with qualified healthcare professionals.

01
Not a diagnosis tool

The service is not designed to diagnose conditions or recommend treatment.

02
Professional care comes first

It is intended to support conversations with healthcare professionals, not replace them.

03
Privacy by design

Personal health information requires careful consent, security and privacy safeguards before any live service is launched.

04
Safety pathways matter

Appropriate escalation and emergency guidance must be clinically reviewed before deployment.
